Vector PDF Tab Maker

Load an already-imposed PDF, assign custom section labels to PDF pages, and export a new vector PDF with white outside tabs and cut lines. The original PDF is not rasterized.

1. Source PDF

2. Tab settings

Tabs automatically fill the available vertical area. Each tab is no taller than the maximum height. If there are too many tabs for one vertical run, the layout starts again at the top in a second tab column and the app automatically adds another tab-width of outside space. This keeps every exported page the same final width.

3. Section labels

Add one row for each tab. Enter the 1-based PDF page number where that section begins and the exact text you want printed on the tab. The tab appears only on that page.

4. Cover pages

Cover pages still receive the added outside width, but they get only the new outer-edge cut line. No tab boxes or tab start/end cut lines are drawn on those four pages.

5. Export

How the cut lines work

  • Every page is expanded on its outside edge: right side for odd pages, left side for even pages.
  • A vertical line marks the original page edge.
  • A vertical line marks the new outside edge.
  • Horizontal lines mark the beginning/end of each tab.
  • The tab area is white and the section text is centered and rotated as a whole.
  • All source PDF text, images, and vector artwork remain PDF content; this app does not render pages to images.
