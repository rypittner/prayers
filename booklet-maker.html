PDF Booklet Maker

Split, select, impose, and create a print-ready booklet PDF without rasterizing the final pages.

1. Upload PDF

PDF.js is used only to create the on-screen previews. The final PDF is built from the original PDF page content using pdf-lib. Preview screenshots are never inserted into the final booklet.

2. Page Splitting

With None, each original PDF page becomes one selectable booklet page.
This only affects the browser preview, not final PDF quality.

4. Booklet Settings

Landscape sheet with two booklet pages side-by-side.
Horizontal: the physical sheet is landscape and the two booklet pages are left and right.

Vertical: the physical sheet is portrait and the two booklet pages are STILL left and right. They are not stacked vertically. For Letter paper this produces two approximately 4.25 × 11 inch page areas, folded longways.
