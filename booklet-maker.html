Margin and gutter behavior
The outer margin is measured from the outside edge of the physical sheet. It is not applied again at the fold. The center gutter is measured separately around the fold. This means increasing the outer margin moves the content inward from the outside edges, while increasing the gutter moves the two booklet pages farther apart around the center fold.
Vertical orientation is intentionally skinny. On Letter paper it creates an approximately 4.25 × 11 inch booklet page on each side. The pages remain side-by-side; they are never stacked.
